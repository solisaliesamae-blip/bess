A little reminder for you

For You, Ry

Don't let other people control your emotions. You don't always have to be too understanding of everyone. Sometimes, you also need to protect your own peace.

Choose your circle of friends wisely. Stay close to the people who genuinely value you, respect you, and appreciate having you in their lives. Not everyone who says they're okay with listening to your problems truly has the same heart as you.

It's okay to open up, but don't overshare your personal problems with just anyone. Learn to know who is truly trustworthy and who deserves to hear the parts of you that you don't easily show.

Focus on yourself and on becoming the person you want to be. Don't let other people's words define your worth. People will always have something to say, but you don't have to let their opinions control your peace.

You're a genuine person, Ry. I hope you always keep that kindness in you. Please don't let someone who doesn't value you as a friend break the good heart you have.

Stay genuine. Stay kind. But remember to choose yourself, too. You deserve people who give you the same respect, understanding, and sincerity that you give them.

Don't lose yourself for people who don't value you.
— A reminder from someone who cares
